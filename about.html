The network

Built by Abdul Muqeet for people who stay till stumps

Muqeet Sport Channel is a sports network: one glass for cricket, football, baseball, hockey, basketball, tennis and Formula 1. The desk does not wait for a single league to finish before the next one starts.

Abdul Muqeet started the channel because the match and the conversation around it belong in the same place — live scores on the strip, a writer in the newsroom, and a player that knows which games are free and which need Premium.
