First Test · Perth

Kohli’s hundred keeps India in the hunt on a spiteful deck

By Ayesha Rahman · Muqeet Sport Cricket · 4 min read

Perth does not do polite cricket. It does bounce, it does glare, and it does a crowd that knows when a batter is hanging on by technique rather than luck. Virat Kohli hung on, then he took over.

India reached tea at 312 for 4. The hundred came with a straight drive that even the bowler clapped. Two chances went down — one at second slip on 28, one at gully on 61 — and Australia will replay both in the dressing room.

Muqeet Sport’s ball-by-ball desk called it the innings of the morning session across every sport we had on air. That is the point of a network: the cricket does not wait for the football to finish.

Cummins still has a new ball in the last hour. If it talks, India will need another fifty from the lower order. If it does not, Kohli will walk off with the kind of hundred that travels.
